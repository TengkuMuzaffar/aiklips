# Example prompts

Open this repo in Claude Code (`claude`) and try these. Claude turns each prompt into `vedit` commands, checks frames visually, and tells you what changed.

## Getting started

- `/new promo ~/Movies/raw-take.mov`
- "Start a project called `launch` with these three clips: ~/Desktop/a.mp4 ~/Desktop/b.mp4 ~/Desktop/c.mp4"

## Cutting

- "Remove all the pauses but keep it natural"
- "Cut the first 2 seconds and everything after 45s"
- "Delete the part from 12.5s to 18s where I stumble"
- "Speed up the B-roll clip 2x and slow down the product reveal"
- "Make jump cuts look intentional"

## Text & animation

- "Add a hook at the top for the first 3 seconds: '3 mistakes killing your sales', bold yellow, make it pop in"
- "Make the title fly in from the left, overshoot a little, then float"
- "Add my logo (~/brand/logo.png) top-right with a gentle bob"
- "At the end, add 'Follow for part 2' in a dark box that pulses"

## Captions

- "Add TikTok-style captions in Malay, 2 words at a time, neon style"
- "Captions are covering my face, move them lower"

## Background & face

- "Remove my background and put me in front of ~/Pictures/office.jpg"
- "Blur the background like portrait mode"
- "Add a glowing cyan face mesh for the intro clip only"
- "Put these sunglasses (~/stickers/shades.png) on my face"
- "Pixelate everyone's face except mine" (tip: blur works per clip; Claude will explain the options)

## History

- "Undo that"
- "Go back to before the captions"
- "Show me the edit history"

## Review & export

- `/review`
- "Export it in full HD"
